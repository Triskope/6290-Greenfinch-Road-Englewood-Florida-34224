The Property

Own the Land. Love the Life.

Welcome to 6290 Greenfinch Road — a spacious, beautifully maintained home on a landscaped corner lot in Lemon Bay Isles, one of Englewood's most welcoming 55+ communities. Ownership here is fee-simple: you own the land, with no lot rent and no monthly maintenance fees.

At 1,534 square feet, this home lives large — a 24-foot living room, a separate family room, a true Florida room, and covered parking for up to four vehicles under a double carport rebuilt to current county code. It comes furnished, with hurricane shutters for most windows and years of careful updates already done.

Pets and golf carts are welcome, public boat ramps are minutes away, and the Gulf beaches of Manasota Key are just down the road.

Discover Englewood, FL

Old Florida, Found

Tucked between Sarasota and Boca Grande on the Gulf Coast, Englewood is the Florida people move here looking for — uncrowded beaches, world-class fishing, and a genuine small-town heart.

Sun & Sand

The Beaches of Manasota Key

Englewood Beach on Manasota Key is just a short drive away — soft Gulf sand, legendary sunsets, and some of the best shark-tooth hunting in Florida. At the key's southern tip, Stump Pass Beach State Park offers a mile of unspoiled shoreline, walking trails, and quiet coves.

Four distinct public beaches line the key, so there's always an uncrowded stretch of sand waiting.

On the Water

Boating & Fishing Paradise

Lemon Bay — a state aquatic preserve — is Englewood's front yard, with public boat ramps minutes from your door. Snook, redfish, and trout fill the grass flats, and the world-famous tarpon waters of Boca Grande Pass are a short run south.

With covered parking for four vehicles and room for a boat at home, this property is set up for the waterfront lifestyle without the waterfront price.

Town & Table

Dearborn Street & Beyond

Historic Dearborn Street is Englewood's charming downtown strip — local restaurants, galleries, boutiques, live music, and a beloved farmers market. It's the kind of main street where shop owners learn your name.
